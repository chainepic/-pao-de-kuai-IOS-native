How to Play (Game Rules)

Paodekuai (跑得快), which translates to "Run Fast", is a highly popular shedding-type card game. The objective is simple: be the first player to get rid of all your cards.

1

The Deck & Players

Typically played with 3 players using a 48-card deck (a standard deck minus the Jokers, one 2, and one Ace, or similar variations). Each player starts with 16 cards.

2

Starting the Game

The player who holds the Spades 3 plays first. The first play must include the Spades 3 card.

3

Valid Combinations

You can play various combinations: Singles, Pairs, Triples, Straights (5+ consecutive cards), Consecutive Pairs (2+ consecutive pairs), Triples with attached cards, and Bombs (4 of a kind).

4

"Must Play" Rule

Unlike some other card games, if the previous player plays a hand and you have cards that can beat it, you must play them! You cannot pass unless you genuinely have no cards that can beat the current play.
