Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 6, 2026

1. Information Collection

We believe in privacy first. The "Paodekuai" application collects minimal information to provide you with the best experience.

  • We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII).
  • Game progress and statistics are stored locally on your device.
  • The app does not show ads, include in-app purchases, or use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

2. How We Use Information

The local data is used solely to save your game state, high scores, statistics, and settings. No data is sent to our servers as we do not maintain accounts or backend servers for gameplay.

3. Third-Party Services

The current version does not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at paodekuai@pokerjudge.com.
